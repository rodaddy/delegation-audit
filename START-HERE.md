# Start Here

You downloaded the Delegation Audit as a zip. All you have to do is get one
file from it -- `installer.md` -- in front of your AI. Find your device below.

## On an iPhone or iPad

1. Open the **Files** app and go to your **Downloads** (or wherever the zip
   saved).
2. **Tap the zip once** -- it unzips into a folder with the same name.
3. Open that folder and tap **`installer.md`** to open it.
4. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the up-arrow) and share it into
   your AI app (the Claude app or the ChatGPT app). If sharing doesn't work,
   open `installer.md`, select all the text, copy it, and paste it into a new
   chat.

## On Windows

1. Find the downloaded zip (usually in your **Downloads** folder).
2. **Right-click it -> Extract All -> Extract.** A normal folder opens.
3. Open your AI in its app or website.
4. **Drag `installer.md` from that folder into the AI's chat box** (or open
   the file, select all, copy, and paste it in).

## On a Mac

1. Find the downloaded zip (usually in **Downloads**).
2. **Double-click the zip** -- it becomes a folder.
3. Open your AI in its app or website.
4. **Drag `installer.md` into the AI's chat box** (or open the file, copy all
   the text, and paste it in).

---

Send that file to your AI and say: **install delegation audit.**
