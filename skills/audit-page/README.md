# audit-page

Stage 4 of the pipeline. `SKILL.md` turns a graded task list into the polished,
self-contained one-page Delegation Audit: what AI carries, what hands down,
what stays the owner's, and the hours it gives back priced at their own rate.

It owns the page template, the `{{FIELD}}` template discipline, branding
resolution, the light-professional default theme, and the print stylesheet.
The `SKILLS_DIR` variable defined here is what the verifiers resolve paths
against after install. Step-by-step procedures live in `workflows/`.
